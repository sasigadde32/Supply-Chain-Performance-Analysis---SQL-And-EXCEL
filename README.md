# Supply-Chain-Performance-Analysis---SQL-And-EXCEL

## Project Overview
An end-to-end supply chain analytics project using SQL and Microsoft Excel to transform operational data into business-focused KPIs, analytical insights, and management dashboards.

The project evaluates supplier performance, product performance, inventory health, revenue concentration, transportation efficiency, and profitability.

## Business Objectives
Evaluate supplier revenue and quality performance.
Analyse product-category sales and profitability.
Identify inventory health and potential stock risks.
Analyse supplier revenue concentration.
Compare transportation modes across cost, speed, and quality.
Develop management-oriented dashboards and recommendations.

## SQL Analysis
### Product Category Performance
Business Question: Which product categories generate the strongest sales performance?
Key KPIs: SKU Count · Total Units Sold · Total Revenue · Average Selling Price · Revenue per SKU
SQL Skills: GROUP BY · COUNT() · SUM() · AVG() · ROUND()

### Supplier Quality & Performance
Business Question: Which suppliers demonstrate the strongest product quality and commercial performance?
Key KPIs: SKU Count · Total Revenue · Average Defect Rate · Pass Rate · Fail Rate · Pending Rate · Supplier Quality
SQL Skills: CTEs · Conditional Aggregation · CASE · Percentage Calculations

### Inventory Health
Business Question: Which inventory groups have healthy stock levels and which require attention?
Key KPIs: Inventory Units · Inventory Value · Low-Stock SKUs · Out-of-Stock SKUs · Low-Stock % · Inventory Health
SQL Skills: Multiple CTEs · JOIN · Conditional Aggregation · CASE · Derived KPIs

### Supplier Revenue Concentration
Business Question: Are suppliers dependent on a small number of high-revenue SKUs?
Key KPIs: Total Revenue · Top 3 SKU Revenue · Top 3 Revenue % · Concentration Segment
SQL Skills: CTEs · Window Functions · DENSE_RANK() · PARTITION BY · Aggregation

### Transportation Efficiency
Business Question: Which transportation mode provides the best balance between cost, speed and quality?
Key KPIs: SKU Coverage · Route Count · Average Shipping Time · Total Cost · Revenue Margin · Defect Rate · Pass Rate · Fail Rate · Pending Rate
SQL Skills: Aggregation · COUNT(DISTINCT) · Conditional Aggregation · KPI Calculations

### Supplier Revenue, Profitability & Quality
Business Question: Which suppliers provide the strongest overall balance of revenue, profitability, quality and cost?
Key KPIs: Total Revenue · Estimated Profit · Profit Margin · Defect Rate · Pass Rate · Manufacturing Cost · Revenue per SKU · SKU Count
SQL Skills: Aggregation · Calculated KPIs · Conditional Aggregation · Profit & Margin Calculations

## Excel Dashboards
### Revenue & Profitability
Executive view of revenue, estimated profitability, supplier contribution, and product-category performance.

### Inventory Health
Comparison of inventory levels, units sold, stock efficiency, and supplier/product performance.

### Transportation Efficiency
Comparison of transportation modes across logistics cost, shipping time, quality, and operational coverage.

## Project Workflow
Raw Data → SQL Analysis → KPI Development → Excel Dashboards → Business Insights → Recommendations

## Key Outcome
The project demonstrates the ability to combine technical SQL analysis with Excel-based business intelligence reporting to answer operational and management-level supply chain questions.
